The service

SpinIn lets you discover the studio schedule, book immersive indoor cycling classes, track your sessions and rides, and receive studio updates.

Your account

Provide accurate information and keep your password secure. You are responsible for activity under your account. The studio may also create an account for you with a temporary password, which you should change after your first sign in.

Bookings and sessions

Booking a class reserves one of the limited bikes and uses one session from your balance. Once a class starts, a booked session is consumed. If your bookings exceed your purchased sessions your balance shows as "owed" and is settled at the studio. Cancellation windows are set by the studio; a class that has already started cannot be cancelled in the app.

Payments

Session bundles are paid in person at the studio. The app records the amount and whether it is paid or pending. Prices and packages are set by the studio.

Class changes and cancellations by the studio

Schedules and classes may change. If the studio cancels a class you are booked into, your held session is refunded to your balance and you are notified.

Your ride stats

Distance and calories you log from the bike console, and figures a linked watch records, are estimates. They power your journey, badges and the leaderboard, and are not medical measurements. Numbers that are not physically plausible for a class are refused.
