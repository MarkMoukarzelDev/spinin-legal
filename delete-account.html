Delete your SpinIn account

SpinIn is the booking app of the SpinIn indoor cycling studio in Beirut, Lebanon. You can delete your account and the data that goes with it at any time, in the app or by email.

In the app

  1. Open SpinIn and sign in.
  2. Go to Profile.
  3. Tap Delete account and confirm.

The account is deleted immediately and you are signed out.

By email

If you cannot use the app, write to hello@spinin.lb from the email address of your account, with the subject "Delete my SpinIn account". We delete the account within 30 days and confirm by reply.

What is deleted

  • Your profile: name, email address, phone number, avatar choice and Instagram handle.
  • Your bookings and ride history, including calories, distance and heart rate figures.
  • Your session records.
  • Linked watch or tracker connections and their access tokens.
  • Your notification token.

What is kept

  • Nothing that identifies you stays in the app's database.
  • Copies may remain in encrypted backups for a limited time until they are overwritten.
  • Records may be retained where the law requires it.

Deleting your account does not remove data held by services you linked, such as Apple Health, Health Connect or WHOOP. Manage that in those services.
